# Klaimee

> Klaimee is a YC P26 startup building certification and financial guarantee for AI agents in production. We certify AI agents through adversarial testing, then underwrite the residual risk with insurance specifically designed to cover the gap that traditional cyber and tech E&O policies explicitly exclude.

## About

Klaimee was founded by Ines Boutemadja (CEO, five years building insurance products) and Julien Catonnet (CTO, AI engineer). The company is part of YC P26.

Liability structure: $2M aggregate / $1M per incident on Tier 1 certifications. Tier 2 (automated adversarial testing + financial guarantee) is in active build with specialty carriers including CFC.
